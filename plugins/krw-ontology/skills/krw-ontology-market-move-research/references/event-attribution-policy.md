# Event Attribution Policy

Use this reference only for `krw-ontology-market-move-research`.

The goal is not to find any headline near a price move. The goal is to decide which explanation is most plausible and investment-relevant.

## 1. Ranking Criteria

Rank candidate explanations by:

```text
materiality to the covered company
timing relative to the move
source quality
company specificity versus market/sector noise
fit with filing-supported business or financial channels
whether the event changes forward cash flow, margin, growth, risk, balance sheet, dilution, or capital allocation
```

Recency alone is not enough. A newer weak headline should not beat a slightly older but more material official or major-news source.

## 2. Classification

Classify the move internally:

```text
company-specific = event clearly maps to the named company
sector-driven = event affects a covered peer group or industry factor
market-driven = broad index/rates/macro move dominates
mixed = company event plus sector/market pressure both matter
unknown = available context does not support attribution
```

Do not expose these labels mechanically. Translate them into Korean investor prose.

## 3. Causality Language

Use careful causality language:

```text
...가 원인으로 지목됩니다
...가 부담으로 해석된 것으로 보입니다
...가 투자심리에 영향을 준 설명 후보입니다
...로 단정하기보다는 ... 경로를 봐야 합니다
```

Avoid:

```text
확실한 원인은...
이 뉴스 때문에 빠졌습니다
시장이 완전히 반영했습니다
이 정도 하락은 정당합니다
```

Only use stronger language when source quality, timing, and company-specific mechanism all support it.

## 4. When Multiple Candidates Exist

If several candidates exist, do not list everything. Group them:

```text
primary explanation candidate
secondary pressure or offset
weak/noisy explanations to discount
```

Then connect only the primary and material secondary candidates to KRW ontology. Do not spend tool budget validating weak noise unless it could change the conclusion.
