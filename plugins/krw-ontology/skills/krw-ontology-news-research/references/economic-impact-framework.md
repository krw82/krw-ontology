# Economic Impact Framework

## When To Use

Apply this framework when the user asks how a specific current event, policy change, industry development, financing action, legal outcome, or macro shock affects a covered company or bounded covered-company set.

Examples:

```text
관세 인상이 반도체 기업에 어떤 영향을 줘?
AI 투자 확대가 알파벳 현금흐름에 어떤 의미야?
수출 규제가 NVDA 장기 성장에 미치는 영향은?
유상증자가 이 회사에 좋은가 나쁜가?
```

Do not force this framework onto a simple request for recent headlines or a short event summary.

## Causal Spine

Use this internal sequence:

```text
1. Current event and evidence status
2. What is genuinely new versus the filing baseline
3. Direct transmission channel
4. First affected business or financial variable
5. Revenue, margin, cash-flow, balance-sheet, or dilution effect
6. Immediate effect versus structural effect
7. Offsetting factor and strongest counterargument
8. Observable confirmation and falsification conditions
```

Every material conclusion must have a transmission channel.

## Direct And Higher-Order Effects

Separate internally:

```text
direct company effect
customer or supplier read-through
sector or competitive effect
financing or valuation-assumption effect
longer-term structural consequence
```

Do not present a higher-order possibility as a direct company fact.

## Current Event Versus Filing Baseline

Use:

```text
news = what may have changed now
latest 10-Q = current reported financial capacity and operating driver
latest 10-K = annual mix, structural business model, and historical risk baseline
```

The event can be newer than the latest filing. Use the filing to test capacity, exposure, and constraints, not to demand that a future event already appear in the filing.

## Financial Channels

Check only channels that matter:

```text
demand, volume, price, mix
cost of revenue and operating expense
margin
working capital and operating cash flow
capex and investment commitments
M&A or strategic investment
debt, liquidity, refinancing, and dilution
capital allocation
```

Keep accounting layers separate.

## Final Answer Shape

Choose headings naturally from the question. For a substantive impact question, a useful shape is:

```text
결론
무엇이 새로 달라졌나
회사에 전달되는 경로
단기 영향과 구조적 영향
가장 강한 반대 논리
다음 확인 조건
```

Do not mechanically create separate `뉴스 근거` and `온톨로지 근거` sections.

Use interpretation-first tables:

```text
| 영향 경로 | 회사에 생기는 변화 | 투자적으로 보는 의미 |
```

End normal answers using the news skill's existing follow-up contract.
